We use the following third-party subprocessors to help us provide and improve
our services:

## Subprocessors with Access to Personal Information

The following subprocessors may have access to personal information such as
email addresses, names, and billing information:

- [Stripe, Inc.](https://stripe.com/) (USA): Payment processing
- [Resend, Inc.](https://resend.com) (USA): Transactional email delivery

## Subprocessors with Access to User Content and Files

The following subprocessors may have access to uploaded files, conversations,
and other user-generated content:

- [Render Services, Inc.](https://render.com/) (USA): Cloud hosting and
  deployment platform

- [RunPod, Inc.](https://www.runpod.io) (USA): GPU cloud computing
- [Portkey](https://portkey.ai) (USA): AI model routing and API services

## Operational Subprocessors

The following subprocessors are used for operational features and typically do
not have direct access to personal user data:

- [OpenStatus](https://www.openstatus.dev/) (France): Service monitoring and
  status pages
- [Plausible.io](https://plausible.io) (Estonia): Privacy-focused web analytics
- [Cloudflare, Inc.](https://www.cloudflare.com/) (USA): CDN, DNS, and security
  services

All subprocessors are carefully selected to ensure they meet our security and
privacy standards. This list is updated as we add or remove service providers.
