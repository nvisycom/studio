Join our remote-first engineering team as a **Backend Developer** and help build the robust systems that power Nvisy's developer productivity platform. You'll be at the forefront of designing and implementing features that fundamentally enhance how developers work, collaborating with our distributed team to solve sophisticated technical challenges.

In this role, you'll have the opportunity to:
- Work with cutting-edge technologies and modern development practices
- Contribute to key architectural decisions that shape our platform's future
- Build systems that directly impact developer workflows worldwide
- Combine strong technical expertise with product thinking in a fully remote environment

We're looking for engineers who thrive on autonomy, value code quality, and are passionate about creating tools that make other developers more productive.

- **Build Developer-Focused Features**: Design and implement new capabilities that enhance developer productivity and streamline complex workflows

### Technical Leadership
- **Tackle Complex Challenges**: Solve sophisticated product and technical challenges, leveraging your systems programming expertise
- **Shape Architecture**: Contribute to key architectural decisions that will define Nvisy's technical future and scalability

### Code Quality & Collaboration
- **Write Quality Code**: Produce clean, maintainable, and efficient code that meets our high engineering standards

### Continuous Improvement
- **Improve Development Process**: Help enhance our development workflows, tooling, and engineering practices
- **Optimize Performance**: Focus on performance improvements and user experience enhancements across the platform

- **Rust Expertise**: Strong proficiency in Rust with hands-on experience building robust, high-performance systems

- **Problem-Solving Skills**: Exceptional debugging abilities and systematic approach to solving complex technical challenges
