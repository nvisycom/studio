## Understanding Cookies

Cookies are small text files that store minimal data, often including a unique
anonymous identifier. When you visit a website, these files are sent to your
browser and stored on your device for future use.

## Our Use of Cookies

At Nvisy, we use cookies and similar tracking technologies, such as local
storage, to monitor service activity and retain certain user information.

We use two types of cookies:

- **Essential Cookies:** Required for the core functionality of our website and
  application.
- **Marketing Cookies:** Used to track user behavior for marketing-related
  insights.

Nvisy uses **only essential cookies**, ensuring the platform functions smoothly
without compromising your privacy.

## Cookies in Use

### Authentication Cookies

When you log in to our platform, authentication cookies maintain your session,
allowing you to navigate between pages without re-entering your credentials.
These cookies are cleared or invalidated when you log out to help protect your
account.

### Payment Processing

We use third-party payment services provided by Stripe to securely process
transactions and detect fraudulent activity.

For more information on how Stripe uses cookies and manages your data, please
refer to their [cookie policy](https://stripe.com/cookie-settings).
