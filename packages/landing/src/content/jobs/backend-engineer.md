Join our remote-first engineering team as a **Backend Developer** and help build
the robust systems that power Nvisy's developer productivity platform. You'll be
at the forefront of designing and implementing features that fundamentally
enhance how developers work, collaborating with our distributed team to solve
sophisticated technical challenges.

In this role, you'll have the opportunity to:

- Work with cutting-edge technologies and modern development practices
- Contribute to key architectural decisions that shape our platform's future
- Build systems that directly impact developer workflows worldwide
- Combine strong technical expertise with product thinking in a fully remote
  environment

We're looking for engineers who thrive on autonomy, value code quality, and are
passionate about creating tools that make other developers more productive.

## Key Responsibilities

### Product Development

- **Build Developer-Focused Features**: Design and implement new capabilities
  that enhance developer productivity and streamline complex workflows
- **Cross-Functional Collaboration**: Partner with designers and engineers to
  create intuitive experiences that solve real developer problems

### Technical Leadership

- **Tackle Complex Challenges**: Solve sophisticated product and technical
  challenges, leveraging your systems programming expertise
- **Shape Architecture**: Contribute to key architectural decisions that will
  define Nvisy's technical future and scalability

### Code Quality & Collaboration

- **Write Quality Code**: Produce clean, maintainable, and efficient code that
  meets our high engineering standards
- **Collaborate in Real-Time**: Participate in code reviews and work closely
  with teammates and community members

### Continuous Improvement

- **Improve Development Process**: Help enhance our development workflows,
  tooling, and engineering practices
- **Optimize Performance**: Focus on performance improvements and user
  experience enhancements across the platform

## What We're Looking For

### Technical Excellence

- **Rust Expertise**: Strong proficiency in Rust with hands-on experience
  building robust, high-performance systems
- **Production Experience**: Proven track record of developing and shipping
  production-grade software that scales
- **Strong Fundamentals**: Solid grasp of algorithms, data structures, and
  computer science principles

### Problem-Solving & Mindset

- **Problem-Solving Skills**: Exceptional debugging abilities and systematic
  approach to solving complex technical challenges
- **Product Mindset**: Strong product intuition with a user-centric approach to
  building features that matter
- **Full-Stack Mindset**: Enthusiasm for contributing to both backend systems
  and frontend experiences

### Remote Work Excellence

- **Autonomous Work Style**: Self-directed work ethic with proven ability to
  operate effectively in a distributed environment
- **Communication Excellence**: Outstanding written and verbal communication
  skills essential for remote collaboration
