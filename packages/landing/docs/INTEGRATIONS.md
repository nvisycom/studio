# Suggested Additional Integrations

Here are some integrations that would complement your current ecosystem:

### Cloud Storage & Collaboration
- **Box** - Enterprise cloud storage popular in healthcare and finance
- **SharePoint** - Direct integration (separate from OneDrive)
- **AWS S3** - For enterprise data lakes and backup systems
- **Azure Blob Storage** - For Microsoft-centric enterprises

### Document Management Systems
- **Clio** - Legal practice management (huge in legal industry)
- **NetDocuments** - Cloud-based document management for legal
- **iManage** - Enterprise document and email management
- **M-Files** - Intelligent information management

### Legal/eDiscovery Platforms
- **Everlaw** - Cloud-based litigation platform
- **Relativity** - eDiscovery platform (noticed you deleted this - consider re-adding?)
- **Logikcull** - eDiscovery automation

### CRM & Business Platforms
- **Salesforce** - Document redaction in CRM workflows
- **HubSpot** - Marketing/sales document handling
- **ServiceNow** - IT service management integration

### Communication & Productivity
- **Email (Gmail/Outlook)** - Direct email attachment redaction
- **Zoom** - Meeting recording and transcript redaction
- **Confluence** - Atlassian documentation platform

### Developer Tools
- **GraphQL API** - Alternative to REST for modern apps
- **Go SDK** - For Go/Golang developers
- **.NET SDK** - For C#/.NET developers
- **CLI Tool** - Command-line interface for automation

### Security & Compliance
- **Vanta** - Compliance automation platform
- **Drata** - Security compliance automation
- **OneTrust** - Privacy management platform

### Industry-Specific
- **Epic/Cerner** - Healthcare EHR systems
- **Veeva** - Life sciences content management
- **Workday** - HR document management
